Amplifier Power OFF bypasses the Pre-Processed Tones. Send the clean DI (bass_di) at 0.0 dB into both bass_low and bass_high. Bonecrusher sits in bass_high slot 1, so the sub band stays clean.

The 180 Hz split

Treat the bass as two instruments: a mono sub and a biting mid guitar.

Why 180 HzDetail
Kick windowKick punch sits at 75–85 Hz, resonance to 100–120 Hz. The sub band holds the bass fundamentals at 49–70 Hz, under the kick punch and the low-mid box.
Guitar chugPalm-mute cab thump lives at 120–160 Hz. A 180 Hz ceiling keeps the sub out of it.
Mono safeBelow about 120–150 Hz the ear can barely localize. Sum it to mono and compress it flat.
Note rangeDjinnBass plays down to G0 in Kontakt naming (C3 = MIDI 60). That is 49 Hz. Bb is 58 Hz. The bass sits in the same octave as the Drop G 7-string. The bass owns 49–70 Hz, so the riff keeps its root. The kick punches above it at 75–85 Hz, and the sc_kick duck clears the bass on each kick hit.
Common rangeSplit points in metal guides run 100–300 Hz. 180 Hz sits inside it.

Zero-latency split & polarity check

  1. Use minimum-phase filters

    24 dB/oct: TDR Nova HP/LP Slope, or two stacked ReaEQ pass bands. Set each stacked band to Q 0.707 (Bandwidth about 1.9 oct). Two Butterworth bands stack into a 24 dB/oct split that sums flat (reasoning). They replace ReaFIR. Zero latency, sharp sub transients.

  2. Play a sustained low note

    Keep bass_low and bass_high both active.

  3. Flip polarity on one band

    Use the track Phase button, or JS: Utility/chanmix2 Left Phase and Right Phase = Invert.

  4. Keep the thicker setting

    Listen at 180 Hz. Pick the fuller result. A pure filter split sums in phase, so a flip would notch. Bonecrusher and the compressors shift phase, so trust the ear test (reasoning).

No FX sends on bass_low

Zero reverb, delay or chorus. Wide lows cause wash and phase cancellation.

Release vs tempo

At 200 BPM a 16th note lasts 75 ms. A ducker release over 80 ms never recovers on fast double kick.

sc_kick into bass_low

  1. Set 4 channels

    On bass_low, set Track Channels to 4.

  2. Feed sc_kick from kick_smp

    Pre-Fader (Post-FX), 0.0 dB. The GGD kick trigger hits at a constant level with no bleed, so the duck stays consistent.

  3. Send from sc_kick

    Pre-Fader (Post-FX). Audio 1/2 to 3/4.

    ReaComp Detector input = Auxiliary Input L+R.

Set the bass level against the vocal

  1. Lock kick and bass first

    Kick and bass should hit together as one low-end event. The sc_kick duck keeps the kick on top.

  2. Dovetail bass top with guitar bottom

    Bendeth matched the bass top end to the guitar bottom end. Where the guitar HPF ends, the bass grit takes over.

  3. Check the vocal last

    Play a chorus. Move bass_bus ±1 dB. Keep the lowest level that still feels heavy. Bendeth: one dB too much bass makes the vocal disappear.
