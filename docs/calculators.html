Decay = note length minus the 1/64 pre-delay, so the tail ends on the next note. Release on the 1/8 or 1/16 lets the compressor recover before the next hit. Tune all of them by ear.

Loudness and streaming

Enter the master's integrated loudness and true peak. The table shows how far each platform turns it down. The site prints at -8.5 to -7.5 LUFS, about -8 LUFS, with true peak at or below -1.0 dBTP.
