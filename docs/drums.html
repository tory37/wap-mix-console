Drums & DRUM BUS

Goal

Punchy kick, cracking snare, tame cymbals. DRUM BUS peaks at -8 to -6 dBFS.

Source setup

Source: GGD Modern & Massive plus Invasion triggers at Max Turbo unverified.

  1. One snare shell

    Keep a single natural snare. No multi-snare stacking.

  2. Sample-align triggers

    Align the Invasion snare trigger to the shell. Ghost notes stay organic.

  3. Skip close-mic gates and compressors

    GGD at Max Turbo already controls bleed and dynamics.

  4. Feed the crush from shells only

    Kick, Snare and Tom acoustic shells. No triggers. No overheads.

KICK BUS

M&M Sum/Sub + Invasion trigger.

  1. 1

    ReaEQ

    REAPER EQ
    HPF
    42–45 Hz, 24 dB/oct (two stacked High Pass bands) unverified
    Punch
    +2 to +3 dB @ 65–75 Hz, Bandwidth 1.0 oct (about Q 1.4)
    Boxiness
    -4 to -5 dB @ 280–350 Hz
    Beater
    +2.5 to +3.5 dB @ 3.2–4 kHz

    The HPF clears room for the 49–58 Hz bass fundamental. The 280–350 Hz cut and the 3.2–4 kHz beater boost match published metal practice (Mynett: cut 250–450 Hz, attack near 3.8 kHz).

    Alternatives SLATEFG-S SLATESD-PE1 low trick: 60 Hz, Boost 4.5, Atten 3.5 unverified JSTJST EQ

    Softness
    Raised (soft knee)

    Beater spikes

SNARE BUS

Single snare + Invasion trigger.

  1. 1

    ReaEQ

    REAPER EQ
    HPF
    100–120 Hz, 24 dB/oct (two stacked High Pass bands) unverified
    Body
    +2 to +3 dB @ 200–220 Hz
    Ring
    -3 dB @ 550–700 Hz
    Crack
    +2 dB @ 2.8–3.2 kHz
    Air
    +1.5 dB @ 8 kHz

    Body and crack up, ring out. The HPF sits higher than a standard-tuning mix. Snare energy below 100 Hz only masks the kick and the Drop G bass. Mynett (Sound On Sound) puts metal snare HPF at 110–170 Hz. Start at 100 Hz and raise it until the body thins.

    Alternatives SLATEFG-S JSTJST EQ
  2. 2

    GClip

    GVST CLIP
    Gain / Clip
    Shave 1.5–2.5 dB
    Softness
    0% (hard clip)
    Target
    Stick spikes
    2x Oversample
    ON

    Essential. A rimshot spike lasts under 2 ms. Shaving it frees headroom with no loss of punch.

    Alternatives JSTJST Clip (adds crack) SLATEHeatwave, Drive 35–45% as a starting point (extra body)

OVERHEADS / CYMBALS

  1. 1

    REAPER EQ
    HPF
    400–450 Hz, 24 dB/oct (two stacked High Pass bands) unverified
    Shelf
    +1 dB @ 12 kHz+

    The steep HPF protects snare phase. Raise the HPF until the cymbals start to thin, then back it off a little. Published metal mixes run it up to 550 Hz.

ROOM BUS

  1. 1

    ReaEQ

    REAPER EQ
    HPF
    150–185 Hz
    LPF
    6.5–7.5 kHz
    Cut
    -3 dB @ 300 Hz

    Keeps the room out of the low end and the cymbal wash. A crushed room at 100 Hz smears the kick under Drop G bass. Mynett high-passes the room at 185 Hz to protect kick punch. If the cymbals still wash, lower the LPF toward 4 kHz.

Parallel & plate returns

SNARE PLATE

  1. 1

    Lustrous Plates

    LiquidSonics VERB
    Plate
    Steel
    Damper (reverb length)
    Short: heavy damping. The Damper has no seconds scale. Set it by ear.
    Pre-delay
    10–20 ms
    Low cut / High cut
    220 Hz / 7 kHz

    Short bright tail that cuts through the guitars. Automate it up in breakdowns. Published metal snare plates run 0.5–0.85 s with 7–11 ms pre-delay. Fast double-kick parts blur with a longer tail. LiquidSonics says real plates run from just under 1 s to about 5 s, so damp hard. If the tail still blurs, shorten it with the built-in Gate. Pre-delay starting point: 10–20 ms. Stretch the tail only in half-time sections.

    Alternatives JSTSky Box, Plate OTHERSSL PlateVerb

DRUM PARALLEL CRUSH

Fed post-fader by Kick, Snare and Tom acoustic shells only.

  1. 1

    VM FG-401

    Slate COMP
    Ratio
    About 10:1 (starting point)
    Target GR
    12–18 dB
    Blend
    Under dry kit, start -14 to -18 dB

    Adds density and sustain. The dry kit keeps the transient. A documented modern-metal crush ran about 18 dB GR, fed by kick, snare and toms, never cymbals. Heavier GR at a lower blend gives density with less transient smear.

    Alternatives SLATEFG-116 Vintage, All-Buttons unverified SLATEVBC FG-MU, -8 to -12 dB GR JSTJW BG-Drums, Pumpin' STOCKReaComp 20:1
