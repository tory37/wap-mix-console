Target sound

Genre
Modern metal. Big, full, melodic, polished.
Tuning
7-string, Drop G to Bb.
References
Breaking Benjamin, Fire From The Gods, Red. Slightly djentier and lower.
Vocals
Howard Jones / AJ Channer style. Clarity first.
Rhythm guitars
Polished JST Toneforge Disruptor.
Arrangement
Orchestral strings in choruses. Aux synths and SFX throughout.

Signal flow at a glance

Two sub-groups sum into print_bus, with the instruments_crushed parallel bus blended in low. It feeds master_bus, which holds the mastering chain. The REAPER master stays empty.
