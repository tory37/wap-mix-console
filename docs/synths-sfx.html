Synths & SFX

Goal

Atmosphere sits inside the shared returns. Built-in synth FX never clutter the low mids or collapse the width.

Synth roles

Give each synth one role. The role sets its band, width and send.

Keep a preset delay only in exposed intros and breakdowns. Cut it everywhere else.

ANA 2 Sidekick ducks the synth on a tempo-synced volume curve. Leave it off on pads by default. The bus already ducks from SC_SPOTLIGHT. Use Sidekick only for an obvious pumping effect.

Width

  1. Pads go wide

    Use ANA 2 Unison Width for spread at the source.

  2. Riff reinforcement synths stay narrow

    The guitars already fill the sides. A narrow synth keeps the stereo field clear.

  3. Lead synths sit center

    Widen them slightly so they leave the lead guitar room.

  4. Check mono

    Set kHs Stereo Width to 0%. The synth folds to mono for a quick check.

Riff reinforcement & fuzz synths

  1. 1

    kHs Distortion

    Kilohearts SAT
    Type
    Overdrive or Saturate
    Drive
    Until the synth blends with the guitars. Starting point.
    Dynamics
    Raise to keep note dynamics
    Mix
    100%, or lower for a cleaner verse

    A clean synth sounds like it is in a different room from distorted guitars. Grit puts it in the same space. Kane Churko and Will Carlson both distort riff synths hard.

  1. Make it sustain

    Overlap MIDI notes or slow the attack. A continuous tone sits under chugs better than a pluck.

  2. Automate the drive by section

    Clean in verses. More drive in pre-choruses. Full drive in choruses.

  3. Low-mid fuzz synths route with the bass

    One compressor on that group, keyed from the kick, ducks fast and almost invisibly.

Arps & plucks

Internal reverb off. Delay Dry/Wet cut and delay High Pass at 300 Hz. Send arps and plucks to STEREO TEMPO DELAY.

Keep them exciting

Starting point: one lead effect per transition. Stack a riser and an impact only at the biggest moment. Vary the samples between choruses. Set levels with item volume, not fader rides.

Risers

Treat risers like pads. Internal reverb 15–25% max. HPF 250–400 Hz. Compression 0–2 dB GR max.

  1. Static HPF plus moving LPF

    The HPF keeps the build off the kick and bass. An LPF that opens across the build adds the lift.

  2. Shave the top of the tail

    Noise risers get harsh at the peak.

  3. Reverse cymbals

    Use a one-shot on its own track. Nudge it so the swell peaks just before the hit.

  4. Downlifters

    Reverse a riser item. It falls instead of rising.

Transition automation

  1. Dip, then hit

    Pull the mix down about 1.5 dB over the last 1–2 bars. Return to 0 dB on the downbeat.

  2. Close a low-pass, then open it

    Close a kHs Filter (Type low pass) on the instrument bus across the build. Bypass it on the hit.

  3. Thin, then fill

    Raise the guitar and bass HPF during the build. Drop it back on the hit.

  4. Narrow, then widen

    Narrow the rhythm guitars in the bar before. Return to full width on the downbeat.

ORCH & SYNTH BUS

Synths share the strings bus: JW BG-Keys 2–3 dB GR, optional soothe2 with its low cut band at 300–500 Hz. The BG-Keys Crush limiter caps synth filter-sweep peaks. Full chain: Orchestral, ORCH & SYNTH BUS.

SFX can run on their own folder into INSTRUMENT BUS. That keeps impacts out of the BG-Keys limiter. Starting point.
