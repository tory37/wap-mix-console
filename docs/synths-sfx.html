Goal

Atmosphere sits inside the shared returns. Built-in synth FX never clutter the low mids or collapse the width.

Built-in FX rules (ANA 2)

Strip or dial down preset reverbs and delays. Every layer then sits in WIDE MIX HALL or STEREO TEMPO DELAY.

Risers, impacts & sub drops

Risers

Treat risers like pads. Internal reverb 15–25% max. HPF 250–400 Hz. Compression 0–2 dB GR max.

Impacts & transitions

Sources give no impact settings. JST Sky Box has transition tools:

Sky Box controlUse
Blood modeAmbient reverb with aggressive modulation, for cinematic breakdown transitions. Its extra control, Bleed Out, modulates the input with white noise.
KillFades out the tail at hard stops and breakdown drops.
FreezeHolds the current tail as a drone.

ORCH & SYNTH BUS

Synths share the strings bus: JW BG-Keys 2–3 dB GR, optional soothe2 with its low cut band at 300–500 Hz. The BG-Keys Crush limiter caps synth filter-sweep peaks. Full chain: Orchestral, ORCH & SYNTH BUS.
