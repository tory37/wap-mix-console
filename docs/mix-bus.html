Setup / 03

Mix Bus & Master

Goal

Glue at 2–3 dB GR. Tone with Max #1 at 0.0 dB GR. Print at -8.5 to -7.5 LUFS with true peak at or below -2.0 dBTP.

Two Maximizers, one polish stage

Two JST Maximizer instances split the work. Max #1 on print_bus is a dark tilt EQ only. Max #2 on master_bus sets loudness from a Rock genre preset.

master_bus is the top folder. It holds print_bus and feeds the REAPER master. Keep the REAPER master empty, at unity.

Soothe2 in Slot 4 is the only dynamic harshness stage. Do not stack a TDR Nova harshness cut on top of it. The Nova on instrument_bus is the vocal duck only.

Park the chain and calibrate it first. Steps live on Gain Staging.

instrument_bus chain

Input target: -4 to -3 dBFS peak. The chain is near unity, so read the peak after it.

The instrument_bus folder holds every non-vocal bus. It gets two utility slots only. Glue, color and clipping happen once, on print_bus.

Not on this bus

No glue compressor: FG-Grey glues once on print_bus. No saturation: VCC / Coffee color the sum. No clipper or limiter: GClip runs on drum_bus and the print bus. No tone EQ: Max #1 tilts the whole mix. No widener. Each one would double a print-bus stage.

Glue in one place

Some metal mixers compress the music bus and leave the vocals out of it. If you move glue to instrument_bus, take FG-Grey off print_bus. Never run both.

Input target: -2 to -1 dBFS peak. See the meter ladder for the summing math.

    Glues drums, bass, guitars and vocals. The detector HPF lets kick sub and snare snap pass. Slate's VBC guide suggests about 2–4 dB GR. A documented modern-metal mix ran an SSL-style bus at 2:1 with 3–4 dB GR on peaks. 2–3 dB keeps glue while Max #2 does the loudness work. If the kit pokes out of the wall, try a faster Attack.

    Macro tilt EQ. A dark curve pushes you toward midrange bite and air without ear fatigue. The JST manual says Tone "adjusts the tone before maximization", so it works at 0.0 dB GR. Zero the other processing knobs so Max #1 only tilts.

  4. 4

    Soothe2

    oeksound RES
    Start
    Factory default preset, all six nodes on
    Low cut node
    Move to 250 Hz. Leave the other five nodes as loaded
    Mode
    Soft (default)
    Depth
    0.8–1.2 max
    Engage

    Catches upper-mid chorus buildup only when it appears. Replaces a redundant TDR Nova. The nodes shape a weighting curve, not separate cuts: below the 250 Hz low cut, soothe2 reacts much less, so it stays mostly off the kick, bass and guitar body.

  5. 5

    GClip

    GVST CLIP
    2x Oversample
    ON
    Gain
    0.0 dB
    Softness
    15–25%
    Clip
    Start at 71% for the -2 to -1 dBFS input (shaves about 1.0–2.0 dB)
    Exact
    Touch point × 0.89 (1 dB) to × 0.79 (2 dB)

    Cuts 1 ms snare and kick spikes. Max #2 then runs cleaner and louder. Clip is a percent of full scale: 100% is 0 dBFS. 71% puts the line at -3.0 dBFS, 1–2 dB under the ladder input. To set it exactly, lower Clip until the line just meets the tallest spikes. Multiply that percent by 0.89 to 0.79. GClip has no GR meter: the gap between the line and the peaks is the reduction. Use the GClip float tool (Alt G).

    Final metal loudness. The JST manual calls Threshold the maximum output peak. It does not say the ceiling is true-peak. A sample-peak ceiling can still produce inter-sample overs. Spotify asks for true peak below -2 dBTP on masters louder than -14 LUFS, and this master prints near -8 LUFS. Start Threshold at -2.5 dB. Then confirm the true peak on a BS.1770 meter.

Auto-Trim for honest A/B

Turn Max #2 Auto-Trim ON during setup. Compare limited and unlimited at equal loudness. Check that the drums keep their punch.

Final print pass

  1. Sweep Max #1 Tone

    Sweep Dark ↔ Bright. GR must stay at 0.0 dB.

  2. Decide on Soothe2

    Engage Slot 4 only if needed. Factory default, low cut node to 250 Hz, Depth 0.8–1.2.

  3. Set GClip

    Clip 71% shaves about 1.0–2.0 dB off the -2 to -1 dBFS peaks. If your peaks sit elsewhere, use the touch point: lower Clip until the line meets the tallest spikes, then multiply by 0.89 (1 dB) to 0.79 (2 dB).

  4. Raise Soothe2 render quality

    In one soothe2 instance, set the offline rendering quality (right-hand column of the quality popup) to 4x Oversample unverified. Then pick "copy to all plug-in instances" in the offline settings menu. Every instance renders at that quality.

  5. Push Max #2

    Raise Maximize to 2.5–4.5 dB GR. Threshold (ceiling) -2.5 dB.

  6. Check true peak

    Put FG-X 2 after Max #2 as a meter. Leave its FG-Comp and FG-Level off unverified. Max true peak must read -2.0 dBTP or lower for streaming. If it reads higher, lower the Max #2 Threshold. A CD or download master may use -1.0 dBTP.

  7. Print the main master

    Read -8.5 to -7.5 LUFS integrated.

  8. Print the instrumental

    Mute vocal_master. Bypass the sc_spotlight duck (instrument_bus Slot 2) and the ducks on fx_inst_plate and fx_inst_dly.

  9. Print the acapella

    Render the vocal branch alone.

Why -8.5 to -7.5 LUFS: it sits inside the reference range. Every platform turns it down, so louder buys nothing on streams. Each extra dB of limiting costs kick and snare punch. Starting point: print at about -8 LUFS. Go no louder than -7 LUFS.
