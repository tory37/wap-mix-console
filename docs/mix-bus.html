Setup / 03

Mix Bus & Master

Goal

Glue at 1.5–2.5 dB GR. Tone with Max #1 at 0.0 dB GR. Print at -8.5 to -7.5 LUFS under a -0.8 to -1.0 dB ceiling.

Two Maximizers, one polish stage

Two JST Maximizer instances split the work. Max #1 on PRINT / MIX BUS is a dark tilt EQ only. Max #2 on MASTER sets loudness from a Rock genre preset.

Soothe2 in Slot 4 is the only dynamic harshness stage. Do not stack TDR Nova on top of it.

Park the chain and calibrate it first. Steps live on Gain Staging.

Auto-Trim for honest A/B

Turn Max #2 Auto-Trim ON during setup. Compare limited and unlimited at equal loudness. Check that the drums keep their punch.

Final print pass

  1. Sweep Max #1 Tone

    Sweep Dark ↔ Light. GR must stay at 0.0 dB.

  2. Decide on Soothe2

    Engage Slot 4 only if needed. Low cut band 250 Hz, Depth 0.8–1.2.

  3. Set GClip

    Shave 1.0–2.0 dB of peaks.

  4. Raise Soothe2 quality

    Switch every Soothe2 instance to 4x Oversample unverified.

  5. Push Max #2

    Drive to 2.5–4.5 dB GR. Threshold (ceiling) -0.8 dB.

  6. Print the main master

    Read -8.5 to -7.5 LUFS integrated.

  7. Print the instrumental

    Mute VOCAL MASTER. Bypass SC_SPOTLIGHT.

  8. Print the acapella

    Render the vocal branch alone.
