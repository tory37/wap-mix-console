Vocals: Lead, Screams, Stacks

Goal

Clarity first. A dead-mono lead core sits in front of Drop G–Bb guitars. BGVs wrap wide around it inside vocal_master.

Every vocal send lands on a return inside vocal_master. Never send a vocal to fx_hall: it lives in instrument_bus, and its vocal tail would stay in the instrumental print.

HPF before Coffee The Pun

Always put an 85–100 Hz HPF before Coffee The Pun. Sub rumble and plosives otherwise drive the preamp model into IMD and pump the compressors.

Clean lead

One chain serves the whole vox_ld_bus. Slots 1–6 are the manual chain. Slots P1, P2 and F come from the guides and are optional.

    JST EQ

    Strips mic rumble and room box before saturation and compression.

  4. 2

    Coffee The Pun

    Acustica EQ / SAT
    Preamp
    ON (Germanium) unverified
    Low 100 Hz Boost
    +1.5 to +2.5 unverified
    Low Atten (opt)
    +1 to +2 unverified
    High 10–12 kHz
    Wide bandwidth, Boost +2 to +4 unverified

    Adds midrange weight and air above the 7.5–9 kHz guitar LPF. Boost plus Atten at 100 Hz scoops 250–400 Hz mud.

    Alternatives SLATEVM SD-PE1
  5. 3

    VM FG-116 Vintage

    Slate COMP
    Ratio
    4:1
    Attack
    3–4 (medium; clockwise is faster) unverified
    Release
    6–7 (fast; clockwise is faster) unverified
    Target GR
    4–6 dB on loud syllable peaks

    Catches consonant transients before the opto stage. The needle snaps back to zero.

  6. 4

    VM Custom Opto

    Slate COMP
    Peak Reduction
    Set for 3–5 dB GR
    Ceiling
    Never above 6 dB GR

    Levels sustain into a steady front-row vocal. Past 6 dB the slow release stacks and the vocal goes dark.

  7. 5

    soothe2

    oeksound RES
    Low cut band
    350–400 Hz
    Depth
    Start 2.5. Raise only while the reduction graph shows real peaks
    Mode
    Soft. Hard only for a fixed peak Soft cannot catch

    Tames 2.5–4.5 kHz throat bite and sibilance. The HPF protects chest warmth. oeksound makes Soft the default: it keeps transients and suits dynamic singers.

  8. 6

    JW BG-Vocals

    JST COMP
    Crush
    3–5 dB GR
    Tone
    15–30%
    Aggro
    10–25% (chorus grit)
    Mix
    75–100%

    Aggro is built-in parallel distortion made for vocals. It adds harmonics that cut through Drop G–Bb guitars. Mark Mynett uses parallel distortion on metal leads for density. Erik Ron, who produced and mixed Fire From The Gods, calls API into a Distressor his go-to chain for vocal bite.

    Alternatives JSTJST BG-Vocals: Bold, Character Med, Comp 30–40%
  9. F

    Fresh Air (optional)

    Slate AIR
    Mid Air
    12% (starting point)
    High Air
    18–22% (starting point)
    Ceiling
    Keep High Air under about 40 (rule of thumb)
    Trim

    Commercial sheen above the guitars. If sibilance rises, add a de-esser after it.

    Alternatives SLATEVM Revival, Shimmer 2.5 (of 40) as a starting point
soothe2 Depth above 3.5

The blueprint allows Depth up to 4.5. The deep dive warns that Depth above 3.5 sounds flat and pushed back. The oeksound manual gives no fixed range. Depth depends on input level and mode, and 3.0 in Soft is not 3.0 in Hard. Start at 2.5. Use Delta to hear what it removes. Go higher only on bright screams.

Scream-only tracks

The JST guide pushes JW BG-Vocals to 5–8 dB GR on dedicated scream tracks. The page now uses it there. Metal mixers crush screams hard: Mark Mynett's vocal arrived with about 20 dB of series compression before the mix. Joey Sturgis puts heavy compression on each vocal track. Keep the shared bus at the manual 3–5 dB.

Hard mode artifacts

Hard mode clamps fast. If the vocal pumps or flanges, switch back to Soft.

Space on screams

David Bendeth put little reverb on screams in Bring Me The Horizon's “Shadow Moses”. He used a 1/8 delay on the screamed sections instead. Choruses got a small room plus a 1/4 delay for length. Starting point: delays repeat in the gaps, while reverb tails sit under the next scream.

Doubles & harmonies

Lead doubles

Do not double every line. Erik Ron skips doubles on many parts. A single lead reads closer and clearer in verses. Save doubles for choruses and big screams.

Lead doubles
Verses
Single lead, dead center
Tucked double
Pan about 1 o'clock (about 30% R), under the lead
Chorus stereo pair
Two doubles, 85–100% L/R, for size without a second singer
Double level
-6 to -10 dB under the lead (starting point)
Breaths on doubles
Cut them. Keep breaths on the lead only (starting point)
Routing
Doubles go to vox_bgv_bus, not vox_ld_bus (starting point)

Bendeth tucked one double near center under the lead, plus a stereo pair. Breaking Benjamin's background vocals on Dark Before Dawn come from the band's other singers, per Ben Burnley.

Delay throws

  1. Automate the send

    Draw send-level automation on vox_ld_bus → fx_vox_dly.

  2. Raise it on throw words

    Lift the send only on the phrases you want repeated.

  3. Let the duck work

    The return ducks 3–4 dB from sc_spotlight. Repeats bloom in the gaps.
